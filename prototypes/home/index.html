How it works

01

Three moves. No overhaul of your life required.

Most people don’t quit in one heroic gesture — they taper, wobble, adjust, and get there. Nicotine Tracker is built around that reality.

  1. 01

    Set your pace.

    Tell it what you use now — honestly. You get a starting ceiling and a gentle step-down schedule. Nothing changes overnight; the plan is allowed to be easy at first.

  2. 02

    Log in two taps.

    Your usual pouches sit one tap away. Strength and totals are tracked automatically, every log is undoable, and offline days queue up and sync later. Recording a pouch never takes longer than using one.

  3. 03

    Adjust with evidence.

    Review when you’re ready, not on a streak’s schedule. See what the week actually looked like, then tighten the plan, loosen it, or leave it alone. Every revision keeps its history.

A closer look

02

Four tools that share one calm interface.

Everything below is drawn from the product itself — same surfaces, same type, same rules. Daily use stays focused; depth opens up when you go looking for it.

01

Your plan

A ceiling you chose, not a score to keep.

A goal in Nicotine Tracker is a daily ceiling — pouches or milligrams — that steps down at a pace you set. Rhythm replaces streaks: the app counts how many days you’ve been at your current pace and remembers your best, without turning one hard day into a reset to zero.

  • Step-down scheduling. Lower the ceiling weekly or hold it steady — both are valid.
  • Revisions keep history. Every past plan stays visible, with what you learned from it.
  • Adjust in place. Changing a goal never wipes the record that got you there.

02

Cravings

Name the urge. Then choose what happens next.

A craving you record is data; a craving you ride out is progress. Log the intensity and what set it off in seconds, take the guided pause when you want it, and either outcome is fine — “used a pouch” is recorded without a frown.

  • A guided pause, always near. One slow breath, timed, before you decide.
  • Trigger patterns. After a few weeks you’ll see your top three — and can plan around them.
  • Ridden-out cravings count. They show up on your day as wins, not gaps.

Your day, honestly

One honest line, not a wall of charts.

A day reads as a single timeline: what you planned, what happened, and the urges you rode out. Enough to answer the only question that matters right now — am I on pace?

  • Planned by you
  • Logged

When it goes sideways

04

A hard day isn’t a failed day.

Some days the plan doesn’t survive contact with real life. Log it anyway. Honesty is the whole practice — the app treats a rough Tuesday the way a good coach would: as data, not as a character flaw.

  • Log it anyway. Two taps, even past the plan.
  • See what really happened. Timing, triggers, the shape of the day.
  • Wake to an adjusted plan. Tomorrow meets you where you are.

Your data

05

Kept like a journal, not a profile.

What you log is sensitive — it’s about your body and your habits. It’s treated that way.

  • Export anytime

    Your full history downloads as CSV or JSON whenever you want it — no waiting, no asking permission, no lock-in.

  • Works offline

    Install it on your phone like a native app. Logs made without a signal queue quietly and sync when you’re back.

  • No ads, no sale of data

    There is no advertising here and nothing about you is for sale. If the product ever charges money, it will be for the product.

  • Delete means delete

    Closing your account removes your logs, plans, and history. Leaving shouldn’t require a support ticket.

Fair questions

06

Asked by people like you.

Do I have to quit completely to use this?

No. Cutting down is a first-class goal, not a warm-up act. Most people start by capping their day and stepping down slowly; some stay at a lower level for a long time. Quitting entirely is one option among several, and the app works the same way for all of them.

What happens when I go over my plan?

It gets noted — not judged. There are no red screens and nothing resets. The day is recorded honestly, and if harder days keep coming, the suggestion you’ll see is to bend the plan to fit, not to try harder at one that isn’t working.

Is it only for nicotine pouches?

It’s tuned for pouches — brands, strengths, quick logging — but anything with a known strength in milligrams can be tracked the same way. The milligram total keeps the record honest even if you switch products.

Will it nag me with notifications?

Only the nudges you ask for. Reminders are opt-in, quiet in tone, and easy to change or turn off. A coach you can mute isn’t a coach worth having.

What does it cost?

It’s free to start, and your logs are always yours to export. If paid plans ever arrive, they’ll pay for the product — your data will never be the product.
